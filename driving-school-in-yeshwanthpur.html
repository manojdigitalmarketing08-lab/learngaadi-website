What decides your fee?

Vehicle type matters, because a scooty, a car and a bus need different time and equipment. The number of sessions matters too, since a nervous beginner needs more hours than someone brushing up. Licence help and manual or automatic cars can also change the plan.

What should be in writing?

Ask for the fee in writing and check that it lists every session. A clear quote also says whether government fees, retests or extra practice hours are included or paid separately.

Will I learn with the same instructor?

Confirm this before you pay any school. Learning with one person each time helps you improve faster, and you should also check that the timings suit your routine.
